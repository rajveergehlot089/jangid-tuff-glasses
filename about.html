About Jangid Tuff Glasses

A dedicated glass company established in Jodhpur, Rajasthan, committed to architectural precision, safety glass solutions, and client-first collaboration.

Our Heritage & Direction

Shaping Spaces With Precision Glass

Jangid Tuff Glasses Private Limited (जांगिड़ टफ ग्लासेस प्राइवेट लिमिटेड) was founded to deliver modern architectural and interior glass products that elevate built environments across Rajasthan. Operating from 21 Narnadi, Boranada, Pal, Jodhpur, we supply builders, designers, and private homeowners with tailored glass solutions.

Modern architecture demands both visual purity and uncompromised safety. We specialize in toughened safety glass that meets rigorous mechanical standards, combined with bespoke finishes like fluted acoustic partitions, high-clarity silver mirrors, backlit LED vanity units, and heavy-duty railing balustrades.

"Our focus is simple: listen carefully to site requirements, advise on the correct glass specifications, and deliver with exact millimeter precision."
