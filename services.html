Specialized Glass Services

Glass Product Consultation

Technical guidance on selecting the appropriate glass variant (clear float, toughened, laminated, or acoustic) matching load parameters and safety building codes.

Our Working Workflow

How We Execute Your Project

A transparent, 4-stage process ensuring accuracy from first contact to glass delivery.

01

Initial Requirement

Share your room photos, architectural elevations, approximate measurements, or product preference via our quote form or WhatsApp.

02

Specification Review

Our technicians evaluate glass type (toughened, fluted, mirror), thickness (8mm, 10mm, 12mm), cutouts, and necessary hardware fittings.

03

Precision Fabrication

Glass is precision cut, edge-polished, drilled, and heat-treated in accordance with certified tempering safety criteria.

04

Dispatch & Handover

Finished glass panels are inspected, securely packed to prevent transit abrasion, and dispatched to your site in Jodhpur or nearby areas.
