Bespoke Engineering

Custom Glass Solutions

Engineered specifically for architects, interior designers, and bespoke builders. We transform complex architectural drawings into precision-cut, tempered, and finished glass installations.

Tailored Craftsmanship

From Concept Drawings to Exact Millimeter Fabrication

Standard glass off the shelf rarely fits custom architectural designs. Whether it is an angled stair balustrade, an oversized sliding partition with recessed hardware, or acoustic decorative screening, we work with your exact CAD specifications.

CNC Drilling & Cutouts

Pre-tempering cutouts for patch fittings, door handles, lock notches, and plumbing clearances.

Edge Work & Profiling

Flat polished edges, bevelled borders, pencil rounds, mitered edges for 45° corner joints.

Specialty Glass Textures

Reeded/fluted glass, acid-etched frosted textures, tinted bronze/grey tones, and acoustic laminates.
