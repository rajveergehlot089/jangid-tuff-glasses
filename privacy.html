Privacy Policy

How we protect your contact information and handle project enquiries.

1. Enquiry Information Handling

When you use the enquiry form on our website to request a quote, the information you submit (including name, telephone number, email, project measurements, and product choices) is utilized solely for preparing your quote and replying to your communication via WhatsApp or telephone.

2. Client Data Security & Zero Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal details to outside commercial third parties or advertising brokers. Your contact details are accessed only by authorized personnel at Jangid Tuff Glasses Private Limited for quotation and order fulfillment.

3. Third-Party Technical Services

This website incorporates external third-party technical assets to provide map navigation (Google Maps iframe) and modern typography (Google Fonts). These services operate subject to their respective privacy standards.

4. WhatsApp Communication

Submitting our website quotation tool opens an end-to-end encrypted chat on WhatsApp. Communication conducted over WhatsApp is governed by WhatsApp's platform privacy policies.

5. Updates and Contact

If you have questions regarding this privacy notice or would like your contact information deleted from our enquiry records, please contact us at 21 Narnadi, Boranada, Pal, Jodhpur, Rajasthan 342017 or call +91 92510 77483.
